# College Chance

A website where a high school student enters their academic profile (GPA, SAT/ACT, state), courses and activities, and sees how they compare to admitted students at any US college, labeled **Reach / Target / Safety** with a one-sentence reason.

Built for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge.

## How to run it

No build step, no install. Just serve the folder with any static file server:

```
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000 in a browser.

**Don't just double-click `index.html`.** Opening it as a `file://` URL makes Chrome and Edge block the app's main script entirely (a CORS restriction on `<script type="module">`, not just `fetch()`), so almost nothing on the page will work. If you do open it that way by mistake, a yellow banner at the top of the page will tell you so and the tabs will still switch (they're wired up separately for exactly this reason) — but search, forms, and documents need the module script, so use a local server.

Once pushed to GitHub, enable GitHub Pages on the repo (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch) and it will be hosted for free.

## How it works

- **index.html** — the page layout: seven tabs (Dashboard, My Profile, My Courses, My Activities, Search Colleges, My College List, My Documents), plus a small inline (non-module) script right after the tabs that handles tab switching and arrow-key/Home/End keyboard navigation on its own — kept separate from `js/app.js` so tabs always work even if that ES module fails to load.
- **style.css** — all styling: the Inter font, color palette, card shadows/rounded corners, Reach/Target/Safety badges, the document checklist, hover/focus states, and responsive layout.
- **js/profile.js** — saves and loads the student's profile and college list in the browser's `localStorage`. No server, no accounts.
- **js/search.js** — loads `data/colleges.json` and filters it by name or state. Also loads `data/majors.json`, matches whatever the student typed as their major ("CS", "comp sci", "Pre-Med") to one of 40 majors, filters to colleges that offer a bachelor's in that major's field, sorts by name / admission rate / biggest program in the major, and writes the "X% of graduates major in …" line for each card.
- **js/dashboard.js** — the rules behind the Dashboard home screen: how much of the 16-unit core plan is completed, which milestones from `data/milestones.json` fall in the next 90 days (using the grade the student will be in on that date, since the school year rolls over in August), the 9th–12th roadmap rows with themes that fit the student's major, and up to 3 "next steps" picked by simple if-then rules from their data.
- **js/grade.js** — the grade options ("8th grade or younger" through 12th) and the grade rules the rest of the app reads: the header badge text, whether GPA applies yet (10th grade and up), and which school years a course can be logged in for each status.
- **js/subjects.js** — small helpers for the clickable Core 4-Year Plan cards and subject pages: each subject's icon, the "Explore" subjects (Arts, Computer Science), the status chip labels, how to word "2 of 4 years," and how to group a subject's catalog courses into Core / Electives / Honors / AP / IB.
- **js/catalog.js** — loads `data/course-catalog.json` and matches whatever the student typed (an official name like "AP United States History" or a common alias like "APUSH" or "NC Math 3") to one catalog course, and builds the course-name dropdown options for a subject.
- **js/combobox.js** — one reusable searchable dropdown ("combobox") used for every autocomplete field (intended major, course name). It wraps a normal text input, draws its own list directly underneath in the app's card style (instead of the browser's dark built-in `<datalist>` popup), filters as you type, supports mouse, touch and keyboard (Up/Down/Enter/Escape/Tab), opens upward when there's no room below, and has the ARIA roles screen readers need.
- **js/activities.js** — saves and loads the activity list in `localStorage`, and holds the rule-based "Ideas to Strengthen Your Activities" logic: a grade-based stage note (explore / go deeper and lead / finish strong), then 3–5 ideas chosen from simple checks: lots of low-hour activities (depth over breadth), no leadership role, nothing tied to their major, no community service, activities without numbers/achievements, and summer plans. Major-specific ideas come from `data/activity-ideas.json`. No AI API.
- **js/chances.js** — the Reach/Target/Safety rules: compares the student's SAT/ACT to a college's typical range, then applies an admission-rate override (sub-20% admit rate is always a reach; over-50% admit rate with an in-range score is a safety).
- **js/documents.js** — saves, loads, and deletes uploaded documents (PDF, Word, or image files) in the browser's `IndexedDB`, since files are too big for `localStorage`. Each document is tagged with a type (Transcript, Resume, Brag Sheet, Essay, Rec Letter, Award, Other) and linked to one or more colleges in My College List.
- **js/onboarding.js** — the first-visit welcome screen's data: saves/loads the student's name, grade, high school, graduation year and major (separately from the academic profile), plus building/parsing the JSON "backup" file for Export/Import.
- **js/tutorial.js** — the 7-step walkthrough's step text (title + description per tab) and the math that positions its tooltip next to whichever tab button is highlighted, clamped to the screen so it never runs off any edge.
- **js/reminders.js** — tracks whether the student has seen the privacy panel, records when the academic profile was last saved, figures out whether a semester boundary (Jan 31 or Jun 30) has passed since then, handles the 7-day "remind me later" snooze, and builds/downloads the `.ics` calendar file with the two yearly grade-update reminders.
- **js/courses.js** — saves and loads the course list in `localStorage`, and holds the course-related rules: which statuses (Completed/In progress/Planned) a student is allowed to log based on their grade, the list of common course-name suggestions per subject, counting how many Honors/AP/IB/Dual Enrollment courses are completed or in progress, and grouping courses by school year with a per-subject count.
- **js/recommendations.js** — the rule-based logic behind the "Suggested Courses" card: counts how many distinct school years a student has logged per core subject and compares that to a recommended 4-year plan; works out the next logical course in a subject (e.g. Algebra II → Pre-Calculus, Spanish II → Spanish III) by matching course names against small hardcoded progression chains; builds the NC-only UNC System minimum-requirements check; flags core subjects still at Regular level as rigor suggestions, prioritized by the student's major; and matches the student's major against `data/course-guidance.json` to suggest courses they haven't logged yet. No AI API involved — it's plain JavaScript comparisons against fixed rules and data.
- **js/app.js** — wires everything together with the page: tab switching, form handling, rendering college cards, document uploads, the per-college document checklist, the welcome/edit-profile overlay, the header greeting, backup export/import, the privacy panel, the semester-update banner, the course form and course list, the Suggested Courses card, and driving the tutorial (highlighting tabs, positioning the tooltip, Back/Next/Skip, keyboard navigation).
- **data/colleges.json** — the college data the app searches: **2,649 real US colleges** from the U.S. Dept. of Education College Scorecard (every school that awards a bachelor's degree and is still operating), including which bachelor's fields of study each one offers and what share of graduates major in each, ~582 KB.
- **data/majors.json** — 40 common majors students type, each with aliases (e.g. "CS", "comp sci" → Computer Science), the federal CIP field families it maps to, and a sample career for the Dashboard (e.g. Computer Science → 💻 Software Engineer, "Computer Science • Technology"), plus readable names for the field families.
- **data/milestones.json** — general, grade-based milestones with their typical month (PSAT, course registration, AP exams, SAT/ACT, building a college list, early and regular deadlines, FAFSA, decision day, and the two semester grade updates). They're typical timing only; the app says to check exact dates with your school and colleges.
- **data/activity-ideas.json** — keyword buckets for intended majors (Computer Science, Engineering, Pre-Med/Health, Business, Law/Government, English/Communications, Psychology/Education, Arts, Environment) with activity ideas, a volunteering idea and summer ideas for each, used by `js/activities.js`.
- **data/course-catalog.json** — about 150 common US high school courses across 8 subjects, each with a short plain-English description, typical grades, prerequisites, which levels it can be taken at, whether it's a lab science, and where it falls in a sequence (e.g. Algebra I → Geometry → Algebra II → Pre-Calculus). Includes every AP course on the College Board's list (checked October 2026) and the main IB Diploma courses at SL/HL. A `notes` list at the top records anything uncertain.
- **data/course-guidance.json** — a small hand-written mapping from intended major (matched by keyword) to a label, suggested AP/advanced courses, and which core subjects are most relevant to that major, used by `js/recommendations.js` to power the major-specific part of the Suggested Courses card.
- **scripts/build_data.py** — the Python script that built `data/colleges.json` from the Scorecard's "Most Recent Institution-Level Data" CSV. Re-run it any time the Scorecard publishes a new release.

## Data credit

College data comes from the U.S. Department of Education [College Scorecard](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) ("Most Recent Institution-Level Data" download). `data/colleges.json` was built by running `scripts/build_data.py` against that CSV, keeping every school with `HIGHDEG` of bachelor's or graduate (i.e. it awards at least a bachelor's) that is currently operating (`CURROPER = 1`), with a name and state on record — **2,649 schools** out of the ~6,278 in the raw file. SAT range is reading/writing + math 25th/75th percentiles summed; ACT range is the composite 25th/75th percentile; admission rate and cost may be missing for schools that don't report them (`js/chances.js` falls back to admission rate alone, or to a generic estimate, when score data isn't available). 

**Fields of study (for the major filter).** The Scorecard has two columns per 2-digit CIP field family (e.g. `11` = Computer & Information Sciences, `14` = Engineering, `26` = Biological & Biomedical Sciences): `PCIPxx`, the share of the school's degrees awarded in that field, and `CIPxxBACHL`, whether a bachelor's is offered in it (0 = no, 1 = yes, 2 = online only). A field counts as "offered" only when both `CIPxxBACHL` is 1 or 2 **and** `PCIPxx` is above zero, because the flag alone is sometimes set for fields with no actual graduates. `PCIPxx` is measured at the school's *predominant* degree level, so the percentage is only kept for the 1,925 schools that mainly award bachelor's degrees (`PREDDEG = 3`), stored as `m: {"11": 21, ...}`. For the other 724 schools it would describe certificates, associate's or graduate degrees, so the app only knows the field is offered (`mo: ["11", ...]`) and says "share of graduates not reported." Field families are broad (Economics and Political Science both fall under Social Sciences), and a bigger share of graduates means a bigger program, **not** a better one. The data doesn't measure quality, so the app never ranks colleges as "best for your major." Adding this grew `colleges.json` from ~358 KB to ~582 KB.

The raw CSV (~100 MB) isn't checked into the repo — only the generated `data/colleges.json` is — since GitHub has file size limits and the app only ever needs the small generated file.
