How This Works & Your Privacy

We never see your information. Everything you enter stays only in this browser on this device. There's no account, no server, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Reach / Target / Safety labels come from simple rules: we compare your test scores to the middle 50% of admitted students at each college (from U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard data), then adjust for how selective the college is.

These are estimates, not predictions. Colleges also look at essays, recommendations, activities and context this app can't see.

Clearing your browser data will erase your info, so use Export Backup (on My Profile) to save a copy.
